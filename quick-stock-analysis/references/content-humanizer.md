# Lightweight Content Humanizer

Use this only as the final editing pass for a completed stock-analysis report.
Do not run a separate audit, score the draft, ask for brand-voice input, or add
new analysis.

## Voice

- Write peer to peer, as one investor explaining the business to another.
- Use plain English, active voice, and short, varied sentences.
- Vary paragraph length. Use compact paragraphs and lists only when they help
  scanning.
- Keep uncertainty honest. Do not hide it behind filler or false authority.

## Edit

- Cut hype, canned transitions, repeated conclusions, and generic filler.
- Remove hedge openings such as `it is important to note` and `it is worth
  mentioning`; start with the actual point.
- Replace AI-heavy wording such as `delve`, `landscape`, `holistic`, `robust`,
  `leverage`, `showcase`, `unlock`, `crucial`, `comprehensive`, `furthermore`,
  and `moreover` with direct language.
- Break up long or uniform sentences. Avoid repeated em dashes and identical
  paragraph structures.
- Prefer retrieved specifics over vague claims. If evidence is missing, keep
  the stated limitation; never invent a specific example or number.

## Preserve

Do not change or add figures, dates, periods, source meaning, classifications,
caveats, confidence, management claims, analyst interpretations, valuation
labels, or the triage decision. Do not add personality through anecdotes,
opinions, certainty, or optimism that the evidence does not support.

Return only the humanized report. Do not append audit notes, scores,
placeholders, process commentary, or a new conclusion.
